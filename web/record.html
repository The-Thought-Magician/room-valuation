Record the room

One video instead of photos. Sharp frames are taken from it to find every item; next you check the list and add close-ups per item.

Room

How to record (30 to 60 seconds)

  1. Lights on, curtains open. Hold the phone upright at chest height.
  2. Stand just inside the door. Turn slowly clockwise all the way round, about 20 seconds for the full turn.
  3. Walk slowly past the desk, shelves and bed, 1 metre away, pausing a second on anything valuable.
  4. Do not zoom. Close-ups of labels and spines come later, on each item's page.
