Step 1 of 3

Photograph the room

The room photos are used to find every item. Next you check the list, then each item gets its own page for a close-up and a voice note.

Room

With tape dimensions the floor area is exact; without them it is estimated from the photos.

Room photos

Lights on. Stand inside the door and turn slowly clockwise, one photo every step, so every object appears at least once: furniture, AC, fan, switchboards, shelves, doors and windows. 8 to 12 photos.

Room video (instead of or as well as photos)

30 to 60 seconds: stand inside the door and turn slowly all the way round, then walk past the desk and shelves. Sharp frames are taken from it and used like room photos. Only video? Use the record-only page.
