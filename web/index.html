Room valuation

Photograph the room, the book spines and any labels, then say out loud what things are. Three sources are combined by Jev into one insured value.

How to capture (2 to 4 minutes)

  1. Room photos: turn once around the room so every object is in some photo (furniture, AC, fan, switchboards, shelves). Lights on.
  2. Close-ups: every book spine (a stack is fine, keep titles readable), and the brand or model label on electronics.
  3. Voice: walk around and say what things are, e.g. "HP Victus laptop, bought last year for 1.9 lakh".
  4. Floor area: type tape measurements if you have them; otherwise the room photos are used to estimate it.
